animeVS: the anime team-drafting strategy game

animeVS is a free team-drafting strategy game that runs in your browser. You build a squad of seven anime fighters, give each one a role, and send the team up a ten-rung ladder of rival squads, or play a friend in a live versus room. There is no account to create and nothing to install.

Battles are decided by the numbers, not by dice. The same team against the same rung always produces the same score, so when a run ends you can see exactly which rung stopped you and build differently next time.

How a run works

  1. Pick Spin or Draft. In Spin the reel hands you one fighter at a time. In Draft every spin shows five fighters and you buy one, with 50 credits to cover all seven picks.
  2. Place each fighter in a role: Captain, Vice-Captain, Tank, Damage, Support, Support II or Healer. Once a role is filled it stays filled.
  3. Lock in and climb. Your team fights rung 1, then rung 2, and keeps going until a rival squad outscores it.
  4. Keep your record. Your best rung, XP and rank are saved on your device, and any result can be shared as a link.

How a team is scored

A team's score starts with the ratings of its seven fighters. Four things are added on top:

  • Role fit. Each role looks for certain tags. Two or more matching tags is a perfect fit (+15), one is a good fit (+5) and none is a misfit. A fighter's signature role always counts as a perfect fit and adds a further +10.
  • Counters. Tags beat other tags, such as range beating melee. Carry more of the winning tag than the rival carries of the losing one and that match-up pays out.
  • Synergy. Pairs from the source material, such as family, mentors and students or sworn rivals, pay a bonus when both fighters are on your squad.
  • Abilities. Every fighter has one. Some add rating in a particular role or on the later rungs, some lift the roles next to them, and some cancel the rival's strongest counter.

Other ways to play

  • Versus: Blind Draft. You and a friend each spin a team in secret. Both teams are revealed together once you have both locked in, and the higher score wins.
  • Versus: Auction. Fighters come up one at a time and you both bid from a $50 purse against a clock.
  • Daily puzzle. One mystery fighter a day, the same for everyone, with seven guesses and a clue for each guess.
