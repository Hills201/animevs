animeVS: the anime team-drafting strategy game

animeVS is a free browser game where you build a team of seven anime fighters on a limited budget, place each one in the role that suits them, and then test the team against a ten-rung ladder of opponents or a friend in a live versus room. Every result is deterministic: there are no hidden dice, so the team you build is the team that wins or loses.

Pick a mode: Draft (spend 50 credits across seven picks), Spin (take what the wheel gives you), Versus (blind draft or live auction against another player) and the Daily challenge.
